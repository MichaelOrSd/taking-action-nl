Sign on paper too

The House of Assembly accepts only paper petitions with original ink signatures, and a petition tabled in the House becomes a public document: the name and address of every signer on the paper pages is available to the public. That is the House's rule and we cannot change it. The online list is different: it goes to the Town and shows the public only initials and community. After you sign online you can print a page in the House's own format with your details filled in, sign it in ink, and drop it off or have a canvasser collect it, if you are comfortable with your name and address being public.
