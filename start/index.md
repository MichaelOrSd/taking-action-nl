# Start a petition

Anyone in Newfoundland and Labrador can propose a petition for this site. Email {{ site.platform.contact }} with the four things below. Petitions are reviewed before they go live so that every one on this site meets the same standard; that protects your petition as much as the site.

## What to send

1. **The problem, in facts you can stand behind.** Dates, places, what happened, who you have already asked for help. Describe what people experienced ("residents report fumes entering their homes"), not conclusions you cannot prove ("the plant is poisoning us"). If a petition makes a factual claim about a named company or person, the organiser must be able to back it up.
2. **Who should receive it, and the ask for each.** A town council, the House of Assembly, a minister, a regulator. Each has its own format. We help with the wording.
3. **Who you are.** A named organiser with a contact email. Groups are welcome; name one person.
4. **Where signers should come from.** The whole province is always welcome. Name the local communities most affected so the count can show them separately.

## The rules we apply

- **House of Assembly petitions** must follow the House's form: the reasons, the prayer ("We, the undersigned, call upon the House of Assembly to urge the Government of Newfoundland and Labrador to …"), and the public-disclosure statement on every page. The House accepts **original handwritten signatures only**. Any MHA may present a petition and none is obliged to. This site prints House-format pages for ink signatures; the online list is for councils and for organising.
- **Town council petitions** have no fixed form in NL. Councils list "petitions" on their agendas and record them in the minutes. A list with confirmed names and civic addresses carries weight.
- **Wording.** Respectful, factual, specific. No personal attacks. No claims about a company's conduct that the organiser cannot support with records.
- **Honesty about effect.** No petition is legally binding. Every petition page says so.

## What we do

We build the page, connect the sign form, print the paper pages, and give you a dashboard to see and export signatures. It costs nothing. You deliver the petition and report back on the page.

## Run your own

Prefer your own site? The platform is open source. Fork [the repository](https://github.com/MichaelOrSd/taking-action-nl), follow the README, and you have your own instance with your own database.
